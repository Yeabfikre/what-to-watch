Every item in this kit is labelled:

- ✅ **Free to reuse**: values (colours, spacing, radii, easing) and open-source fonts and icons. Install them from their official source.

- 🎨 **Style only**: belongs to the site's owner. Recreate the feel (shape, weight, colour treatment); never copy the file. Its file is in `reference/`: see *Reference files* below for when you may use it.

## Reference files (`reference/`)

133 file(s): exact copies of what the source page used. Its markup (with its words) and stylesheets, its SVG drawings, pictures, font files and animation data, kept so an agent can reproduce the page faithfully instead of approximating it. They still belong to the source site's owner. Use them where you have the right to: your own site, a licensed template, or a client who gave you the files. Otherwise keep the *look* and replace the files. A font marked 🔑 above needs a licence even though its file is here.

Frames in `frames/` have images replaced by flat blocks and text replaced by bars. Apart from `reference/`, this kit contains no font files, images, logos, stylesheets or text from the source site.
